A five-year study following 1,000 people with vitiligo and their families to find what sets it off — and who it may be coming for next. Everything happens at home.

What the VIGOR study is

Vitiligo runs in families, but no one can yet say why one sibling develops it and another never does. VIGOR follows people with vitiligo alongside their close relatives who don't have it, collecting the same samples from both over five years.

When someone in the study develops vitiligo, we can look back at what their skin and blood were doing in the months before it appeared. That comparison is the whole point, and it only works if enough unaffected family members take part.

Unlocking the secrets of vitiligo. Not a treatment trial. No drug is being tested. This study exists to find causes.

Who can join?

  • Anyone age 6 or older with vitiligo, anywhere in the United States or Canada.
  • Their close relatives without vitiligo — parents, siblings, children, cousins.
  • The study needs more participants without vitiligo than with it. If you have vitiligo, the most valuable thing you can do is invite your family.
  • No treatment history required. You can be newly diagnosed or living with it for decades.

What's involved?

  • Enroll in the VIGOR app and give one saliva sample to start.
  • A survey every three months. About ten minutes.
  • Skin and blood samples every six months, collected at home from kits we mail you.
  • Five years. No clinic visits, no travel, no cost to you.

Who we are

VIGOR is run by dermatologists, geneticists and study coordinators at UMass Chan Medical School, working with investigators across Mass General Brigham. Several of us have spent our careers on vitiligo specifically.

You will not be a record number to us. The same small team reads your surveys, ships your kits and answers your email for the next five years.

Who supports this work

VIGOR is funded by the National Institutes of Health and coordinated at UMass Chan Medical School. It was designed as a siteless study from the beginning, so that families in every state could take part without living near a research hospital.

Samples are handled under the study's approved protocol, and your genetic data is stored separately from anything that identifies you. Participants can withdraw at any point without giving a reason.

Questions people ask first

Is this a treatment study?

No. Nothing is being tested on you and no medication is given or withheld. VIGOR is an observational study: we collect samples and surveys to understand what triggers vitiligo. You keep seeing your own doctor and continue any treatment you are already on.

Does it cost anything?

No. Kits are mailed to you and return shipping is covered. There are no visits to pay for and no insurance involved, because nothing here is billed as medical care.

Do I have to travel or visit a clinic?

Never. Every part of the study happens where you are — surveys in the app, samples collected at your kitchen table with devices designed to be used without training. Participants take part from more than forty states.

What happens to my samples and my genetic data?

Samples are labeled with a study code, not your name. Genetic data is stored separately from the information that identifies you and is used only for vitiligo research under the study's approved protocol. It is not sold, and it is not shared with insurers or employers.

Can my child participate?

Yes, from age six. A parent or guardian consents and helps with collection; older children can give their own assent. Children without vitiligo whose sibling or parent has it are among the most important participants in the study.

What if I can't keep up with it for five years?

Life happens, and the study is built for that. Missing a survey or a collection window does not remove you. If you decide to stop entirely, you tell the coordinator and that is the end of it — no explanation needed.
